28 verses · Durgā Saptaśatī, chapter 5

या देवी सर्वभूतेषु

Devī Stuti (Yā Devī)

The hymn of the gods to the Goddess from chapter 5 of the Durgā Saptaśatī (Devī Māhātmya). After five verses of salutation comes the famous yā devī sarva-bhūteṣu: twenty-one verses naming the forms in which the Goddess dwells in all beings, each ending namas tasyai namas tasyai namas tasyai namo namaḥ.

नमो देव्यै महादेव्यै
शिवायै सततं नमः।
नमः प्रकृत्यै भद्रायै
नियताः प्रणताः स्म ताम्॥

Growth of Those Who Bow
