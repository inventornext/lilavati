Gita Chapter 15: Purushottama Yoga · Bhagavad Gita 15.3

Cut It with Detachment

न रूपमस्येह तथोपलभ्यते
नान्तो न चादिर्न च सम्प्रतिष्ठा।
अश्वत्थमेनं सुविरूढमूल-
मसङ्गशस्त्रेण दृढेन छित्त्वा॥

अर्थ · Meaning

Its real form is not seen here, nor its end, its beginning or its base. Cutting this deep-rooted tree with the strong axe of detachment...
