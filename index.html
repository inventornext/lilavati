Learn Sanskrit through Bhaskaracharya's mathematics. One verse at a time: the original text, every word, the grammar, and the solution worked out.

In 1150 CE, Bhaskaracharya (Bhāskara II) wrote the Līlāvatī, the arithmetic and geometry volume of his Siddhānta Śiromaṇi. Its problems are written as Sanskrit poetry, in proper classical meters, often addressed to a young girl named Lilavati. That makes it a perfect textbook for learning the language and the mathematics together.

How to use this page: read the verse aloud, then try to understand it from the word cards before reading the translation. Try the puzzle yourself before opening the solution.
